## I. The Door

It sticks in summer
and in winter it forgets
how heavy it is. Either way
        you have to lean
into it, the way you lean
into an argument you have already lost.

## II. The Window

Everything I know about weather
I learned from this glass:
        how rain arrives sideways,
                how snow
        takes its time,
how the sun comes in
like a guest who has been here before
and knows where the cups are.

## III. The Kettle

It sings before it is ready.
I have been that kind of thing.

## IV. The Stairs

Thirteen of them.
The fourth one speaks.
I learned to step over it
coming home later than I said,
and I step over it still,
though no one is listening
        and no one is awake
                and no one is waiting up.

## V. The Bed

Half of it is a country
I visit on Sundays.
The rest is where I keep
the shape of sleeping,
the long sentence
        of one body
                learning
                        to take up
                                the whole page.

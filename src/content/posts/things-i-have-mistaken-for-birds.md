A paper bag rising off the pavement, hesitating, deciding against the sky.
A glove on a railing, its fingers spread as if it had only just landed.
My mother's hands at the sink, two pale things startled by the water.
The shadow of a plane crossing the field faster than anything with wings.
A leaf. Many leaves. The whole of October, really.
A kite with its string cut, still pretending.
The sound of the gate at night, which was the hinge, which was the wind, which was no one.
A woman on the platform throwing up her arms as the train pulled out, and then not putting them down.
The word *later*, which flew off every time I said it.
Myself, once, at the top of the stairs, before I knew anything about falling.

And once, only once, a bird,
which I mistook for nothing,
        and let go.

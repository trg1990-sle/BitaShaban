the first drop
        is a rumour

                the second        a rumour confirmed

then the street
        takes up the story
                and tells it
                        to every gutter
                                in the city

        listen
                    listen

the roofs are reading aloud
        in a language of tin
                and the window
                        is a page
                                someone keeps
                                        turning
                                                and
                                                        turning

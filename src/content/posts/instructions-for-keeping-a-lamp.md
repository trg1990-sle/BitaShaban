Keep it low. A lamp is not the sun
and does not want to be.

Let it be the last thing you put out
and the first thing that remembers you.

When the power fails, forgive it.
It was only ever borrowing.

Clean the glass with yesterday's paper
so the light comes through the news.

Don't read by it too long.
It will start to read you back.

And if you leave the house for good,
leave it on. Let the street believe

someone is still awake in there,
turning a page, refusing the dark.

The ink goes on the colour of a bruise
that hasn't happened yet: a pale
uncertain blue, almost not there.

Give it an hour. Give it the air.
It darkens as it learns the page,
the way a word does, said enough.

By morning it is black and sure of itself.
In a hundred years it will be brown,
the colour of a leaf that kept its shape.

I write to you in what will change.
Read it soon, while it is still
becoming what I meant.

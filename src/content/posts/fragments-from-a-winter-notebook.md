The radiator knocks
like someone who has
the wrong house.

Snow on the railings.
Every fence in the city
underlined.

My breath leaves before I do.

January:
the month that reads
the last page first.

Under the ice
the river keeps talking
the way voices do
in the next room,
        not quite to us.

I wrote your name on the window
and the window
        wept it away.

A crow on the wire,
folded like a letter
no one will open.

Even the light
comes in late
and leaves early,
like a guest with somewhere better to be.

Spring, they tell me.
I have heard that one before.
